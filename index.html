BEGINNER'S GUIDE TO BASKETBALL



BASIC RULES IN BASKETBALL
  • Two teams with 5 players
  • No traveling
  • Free throw before the line
  • Wait for the referee's whistle
BASKETBALL EQUIPMENT
  • Ball
  • Basketball shoes
  • Jersey Shirt
  • Shorts
BASIC TECHNIQUES
  • Dribbling
  • Dunking
  • Rebounding
  • Shooting
